Privacy Policy for Flip Trap

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flip Trap ("the Game"), developed by Stone's AI Lab ("we", "us"), handles information when you play it.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The Game has no accounts, sign-ins, or chat, and it doesn't send any analytics or tracking data to us or to any third party we operate.

Game progress and settings (such as high score and sound settings) may be saved locally in your browser (for example, via localStorage). This data stays on your device, we can't access it, and you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Platforms and advertising

The Game is distributed through third-party game platforms (such as Poki). These platforms may show ads and process data on their own under their own privacy policies, which we don't control. Please refer to the platform's privacy policy:

  • Poki Privacy Policy: https://poki.com/en/c/privacy-statement

Children's privacy

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region).

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy (for example, if we add analytics), we'll post the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date.
